Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This policy explains what data 21 Table Blackjack ("the Game", "we", "us") collects when you play, and how it's used. This is a free-to-play game using virtual currency only — there is no real-money gambling, and virtual chips cannot be exchanged for cash or anything of monetary value.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you first open the game, we create an anonymous account tied to your device/browser (via Firebase Authentication's anonymous sign-in). This is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identity unless you separately choose to purchase something.
  • Gameplay data. Your in-game bankroll, purchase history, and preferences (e.g. sound settings) are stored against that anonymous account.
  • Payment information. If you make a purchase, payment is processed entirely by Stripe (web) or Google Play Billing (Android) — we never see or store your card number. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, and which product was purchased.
  • Advertising data. If ads are shown, our ad partner (Google AdSense/AdMob, via the Ad Placement API) may collect data as described in Google's Partner Sites policy, which may include device identifiers and general location, used to serve and measure ads.
  • Basic technical data. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be collected automatically by our hosting provider.

How We Use Information

  • To operate the game and save your progress/purchases
  • To process and verify purchases
  • To display advertising (where applicable) and measure ad performance
  • To maintain security and prevent fraud/abuse

Third Parties We Use

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and backend hosting
  • Stripe — web payment processing
  • Google Play Billing — Android payment processing
  • Google AdSense / AdMob — advertising, where shown

Each of these third parties has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We encourage you to review: Google's Privacy Policy and Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

  • Ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google's Ads Settings, or on Android via your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Data deletion. Since your account is anonymous, clearing your browser data (or uninstalling the app) effectively disconnects you from your saved progress. To request deletion of purchase records associated with your account, contact us using the details below with your anonymous user ID (visible in-game, if we add a way to display it) or purchase receipt.

Children's Privacy

This game is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

EEA / UK / Switzerland Users (GDPR)

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before any non-essential cookies or ad personalization data collection occurs, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can withdraw consent at any time via the consent management link shown on the site, or via Google's Ads Settings.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" (as those terms are defined by California law) — which for a site using advertising may include ad-related data sharing. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
